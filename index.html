About

Where maps

I build products that turn geographic data into decisions. My work sits at the intersection of Earth observation, deep learning, and generative AI — applied to some of the most consequential problems in risk and sustainability.

At Swiss Re, I lead the development of geospatial AI solutions that help insurers understand natural hazard exposure anywhere on Earth. Before that I built risk analytics tools at WTW and gained deep platform expertise at Esri.

My expertise spans the full stack — from raw satellite imagery processing to model deployment, from geopolitical risk scoring to ESG reporting frameworks. I don't just understand the technology; I understand the business decisions it needs to drive.
