About

I turn satellite imagery and geographic data into decisions that matter — for climate risk, reinsurance, and the planet.

I build products at the intersection of Earth observation, deep learning, and generative AI — applied to some of the most consequential problems in risk and sustainability.

At Swiss Re, I lead the development of geospatial AI solutions that help insurers understand natural hazard exposure anywhere on Earth. Before that I built proprietary risk tools at WTW and developed deep platform expertise at Esri.

My work spans the full stack — from raw satellite imagery processing and model deployment to geopolitical risk scoring and ESG frameworks. I understand not just the technology, but the decisions it needs to drive.
